The head-unit runtime is already in this directory:

  uxplay                  wrapper (sets LD_LIBRARY_PATH and GST_PLUGIN_PATH)
  uxplay.bin              UxPlay built on Ubuntu 18.04, glibc 2.27, x86-64
  gst-plugin-scanner      GStreamer 1.14 plugin scanner from the same system
  lib/                    shared libraries the binary needs, except glibc
  lib/gstreamer-1.0/      plugins: decodebin, h264parse, avdec_h264, videoconvert,
                          waylandsink, alsasink, level, appsink and their helpers

glibc, libm, libpthread, libdl, librt and the dynamic linker stay on the
head unit. Do not replace them. Library names and the Ubuntu 18.04 packages
they came from are in LIBS.txt.

The service starts uxplay while the app writes airplay into
/run/g21-session/allow. It prefers the car Wi-Fi AP at 172.16.222.97 so the
iPhone can join as a client and see G21. An iPhone Personal Hotspot uplink
alone is rejected for discovery (phone-is-ap): the phone hosting that hotspot
does not browse AirPlay receivers on its own client subnet.
